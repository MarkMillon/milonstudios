915 MHz Class-E PA Match

Harmonic-balance sweep for a logistics asset-tracking client's LoRa front-end

SCHEMATIC
SIMULATION OUTPUT

A two-stage output match for a CMOS PA driving 50Ω, designed to hit 17 dBm at 90% drain efficiency across the US 902–928 MHz ISM band. Qucs-S handled the load-pull contours and the harmonic-balance simulation of the Class-E waveform shaping for the client's next-gen tracker.

Topology
Class-E · L-network → π-filter
Sim type
HB · 5 harmonics · 50 grid pts
Target P_out
17 dBm @ 3.3V supply
Result
ηD = 88.4% · 2H rejection 28 dB
Challenge The shunt capacitance for Class-E operation is parasitic-sensitive — the 0402 cap parasitic L was actually larger than the design value at 915 MHz. Migrated to an interdigitated cap drawn into the layout and re-simulated using imported S-parameter touchstone of the PCB segment.
Outcome Measured efficiency on the client's first EVT hardware: 84% vs. simulated 88%. Within expected loss budget for the chosen substrate (FR-4, tan δ ≈ 0.02). Now in their pre-production tracker carrier.

MS-L-005 CLOSED 2025-10

Anti-Alias Filter — 5th Order Bessel

Pre-ADC filter design for a 96 kHz AK5552 capture chain

SCHEMATIC
SIMULATION OUTPUT

A 5th-order Bessel low-pass at 40 kHz, implemented with two cascaded OPA1612 stages plus a passive RC. Optimized using LTspice's Monte Carlo for component tolerance sensitivity — passive values are E96 1% and op-amp offset modeled to AVT-typical distributions.

Topology
Sallen-Key + MFB cascade · OPA1612
Cutoff
40 kHz · group delay flat to 30 kHz
Sim
200-run Monte Carlo · ±1% R, ±5% C
Worst-case
Δ phase = ±2.1° at 20 kHz across MC runs
Challenge Bessel response is sensitive to capacitor tolerance — a single 10% cap blew the group-delay flatness target. Specified C0G dielectric on the three frequency-setting caps to hold ±2%.
Outcome Yield model showed 97.5% of boards within spec without tuning, acceptable for the 50-unit production run.
