The short version

  • Your study data stays on your device
  • The AI Coach runs privately on your device
  • No accounts, no servers, no tracking

Patent Bar Prep keeps your progress on your device. We don't run servers, we don't require an account, we don't track you, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

What information the app handles

Patent Bar Prep is a study app for the USPTO registration examination. To track your progress, the app keeps:

  • Your study progress: the answers you choose, which questions you've mastered or missed, bookmarks, and the days you studied (for your streak).
  • Your preferences: settings such as hiding pre-AIA questions, light or dark appearance, and, if you choose to enter it, your exam date.
  • Coach memory: short notes of the topics you've asked the AI Coach about, so it can tailor its help.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

The AI Coach

The AI Coach uses Apple Intelligence, which runs on your device. Your questions to the coach and its answers are processed locally and are not sent to us or to any third-party AI service.

Where the information is stored

All of the information above is stored only on your device. Patent Bar Prep does not have a backend server and does not upload, sync, or back up your data to any external service we operate.

If you back up your iPhone or iPad with iCloud Backup or to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in that backup, under your Apple account and settings. We have no access to those backups.

If you delete the app, your study data is deleted with it, and we cannot recover it.

Purchases and subscriptions

Patent Bar Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment and manages any subscription. We never see your payment details. The app checks with Apple whether you have an active purchase so it can unlock Pro features. Purchases are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Third-party services

Apart from Apple's App Store and Apple Intelligence described above, Patent Bar Prep does not use any analytics services, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or other third-party SDKs.

Tracking

Patent Bar Prep does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or similar technologies.

Your controls

  • Reset your progress from Settings → Reset All Progress. This erases your answers, mastery, bookmarks, streak, and what the coach remembers.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID's subscription settings.
  • Delete the app from your home screen. iOS removes the app and its local data together.

Children's privacy

Patent Bar Prep is intended for adults preparing for a professional licensing exam. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and post the revised policy at this URL.
