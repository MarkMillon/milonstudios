Draw Russian privacy policy

How the Draw Russian iOS app handles your information. The short answer: we don't collect any personal information, and your progress stays on your device.

  • Effective October 15, 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no login
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking
  • Your progress stays on your device

Your progress, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. Purchases are handled by Apple. A service called RevenueCat checks whether your subscription is active, using a random ID that isn't tied to your name or email.

Information we collect: none

We do not collect, store, or receive personal information about you. Specifically, Draw Russian does not collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address or phone number
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, or other files
  • Advertising identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA) or other identifiers used to track you
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or information about how you use the app

Draw Russian contains no analytics tools and no advertising, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves a few things on your device only:

  • Your letter progress and practice history
  • Your streaks
  • Your app settings

This data is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. The home screen widget reads the same on-device data to show your progress; it doesn't send it anywhere. This data is never sent to us. We have no server that stores it, and we can't see it.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include this data in your device backup. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Deleting the app deletes this data from your device.

Notifications

Practice reminders are optional. They are only turned on if you allow them, and they are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in your device's Settings.

Purchases

If you buy a subscription or in-app purchase, Apple processes the payment through the App Store. We never see your payment card or billing details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

To check whether your purchase is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app and your App Store purchase receipt. It does not receive your name or email address from us. RevenueCat's handling of this information is described in RevenueCat's privacy policy.

This purchase check is the only time the app communicates with an outside service.

Children's privacy

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app has no accounts, no chat, no ads, and no analytics, so there is no way for a child to share personal information with us through the app.

If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

We keep nothing about you on our servers. To delete everything the app has stored, delete the app from your device.

RevenueCat keeps a record of the anonymous app user ID and purchase history so your purchase can be restored. That record isn't linked to your name or email. To manage or cancel a subscription, go to your Apple account's subscription settings on your device.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the updated version at this URL and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email us at privacy@milonstudios.com.
