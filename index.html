01 The problem

The chains have apps. You don't.

Every Starbucks regular has the Starbucks app. Every Sweetgreen customer has the Sweetgreen app. They open it, tap their order, walk in, pick it up. Their loyalty points pile up. They come back more often, spend more per visit, and never have to remember a phone number.

Independent shops have been locked out of this — not because the technology is hard, but because building a real app has cost six figures and six months. Until now.

02 What it does

A real app. Yours.

iOS and Android, branded entirely to your shop. Your colors, your menu, your logo on every customer's home screen. Not a white-label template that twenty other shops are using — yours.

Loyalty

Stars, points, free drinks.

Reward regulars automatically. No punch cards to lose, no phone numbers to type. Customers earn just by ordering.

Mobile ordering

Skip the line.

Order ahead, pay in-app, pick up at the counter. Frictionless for them, faster throughput for you.

Push notifications

Reach customers directly.

New seasonal drink? Rainy-day promo? Send a notification. No Instagram algorithm in the way.

Customer list

The data is yours.

Names, emails, order history, preferences — owned by you, not by a third-party platform that could change the rules tomorrow.

03 Why it works

Regulars buy more, more often.

The math is simple. A loyalty member visits more often than a one-time customer. They spend more per visit. They tell their friends. An app on their phone makes them a member without the friction of a paper punch card or a "what's your phone number" awkward exchange at the register.
